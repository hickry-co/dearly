# Dearly

*A cozy scrapbook journal for iPhone.*

Every day gets its own page. Write, paste, stick and decorate, then keep it all in a notebook that looks and feels like yours.

## What you can do

- **Decorate** each day with stickers, cut-out letters, washi tape, clips, polaroids, handwritten notes and your own cut-out stickers
- **Arrange** everything by dragging, pinching and rotating, with 45 and 90 degree snapping, layers, groups and favorites
- **Add a mood and places** to a page: a heart sticker and a little airmail postcard map
- **Make notebooks yours** with leather, craft-paper or chipboard covers, bands, cords and twine, charms in gold, brass or silver, and text pressed into the cover
- **Keep them private** with a password or Face ID per journal
- **Look back** on a calendar of stamps and moods, with statistics and Home Screen widgets
- **Keep it safe** with backups and optional iCloud sync, in English and Turkish

## Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open an issue: [github.com/hickry-co/dearly/issues](https://github.com/hickry-co/dearly/issues)

## Privacy

Your journals stay on your device. iCloud sync is optional and off by default. Dearly has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Location is used only when you add a place to a page.
